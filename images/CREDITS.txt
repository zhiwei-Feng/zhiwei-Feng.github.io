Derived from "The Tower of Babel" by Gustave Doré (1832–1883), a wood
engraving from "La Sainte Bible" (1866). Depicts the unfinished tower
rising into clouds with diminutive figures and architecture at its base —
the dwarfed-figure / monumental-ruin composition characteristic of
Romantic biblical illustration.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Dore_Bible_The_Tower_of_Babel.jpg
Public domain (artist died 1883; copyright expired worldwide).

Modifications: center-cropped to square from the original vertical plate,
auto-contrast normalized, inverted (dark ground / light line), thresholded
to keep only the darkest original strokes as bright silhouette over a
near-black field, downscaled to 1000x1000, encoded as lossy WebP. No
restriction on further use.
